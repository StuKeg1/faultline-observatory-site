Claim scope/target matters. Historical reconstruction overlays are specific to individual assessments, not automatic authority for later assessment codes. ## C. Locator-based findings and dispositions

### A03-01 — Two current COLLAPSED / VS-05 codes lack a demonstrated operation warrant in the latest assessment [MATERIAL; HIGH confidence in warrant gap]
- **FR-AM-0001** [record](https://faultlinewatch.com/the-record/fr-am-0001/), [canonical module](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/data/records/FR-AM-0001.js), AS-003–AS-005. AS-003's historical stored VS-05 was ratified *interpreted VS-04* in July overlay. However current **AS-005** dated 2026-09-08 remains **COLLAPSED / VS-05**. Its claim is fusion autonomously produced by room-temperature electrochemical cells. AS-005 explicitly says the 2025/2026 evidence is **externally ion-beam-driven fusion**, not reproduction/operation of the claimed cell process. No affirmative operational warrant for the full claimed capability was identified in AS-005. The July review's correction does not mechanically apply to AS-005.
- **FR-AM-0005** [record](https://faultlinewatch.com/the-record/fr-am-0005/), [canonical module](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/data/records/FR-AM-0005.js), AS-001–AS-004. July overlays reinterpret AS-001 and AS-002 from stored VS-05 to VS-04, while the **latest AS-004** dated 2026-10-30 still states **COLLAPSED / VS-05**. It records seven unsuccessful La–Sc synthesis attempts and expressly refuses to treat them as a complete universal null. No successful operated room-temperature-superconducting claimed material is established in its current summary.
- **Competing argument:** the “operation” label may have been retained as shorthand for an extensively exercised testing system, or historical stage inheritance, not a positive scientific finding. This is precisely the explanation rejected by July authority; it is not itself evidence of the claimed capability.
- **Disposition: ADOPT bounded present-day *warrant review*** of AM-0001 AS-004/005 and AM-0005 AS-003/004, compare source-level admitted evidence and change-kind lineage; prepare explicit keep/change options, with **VS-04 as a candidate only, not a ratified outcome**. Preserve historical interpreted overlays and every assessment. Any stage change requires a distinct operator decision and append-only current assessment/mutation, not a bulk patch. Do **not** automatically flip the two records or reinterpret COLLAPSED as a stage.

### A03-02 — AI imaging VS-05 disputed in an existing formal case challenge [MATERIAL; MEDIUM confidence in unsettled boundary]
- **FR-AI-0008** [module](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/data/records/FR-AI-0008.js), latest AS-004 2026-10-18 **FRAGMENTING / VS-05**, `VSC-001.status = awaiting_operator_decision`. - The completed MASAI trial provides *applied clinical pathway evidence*, not merely a device marketing authorisation. The opposing uncertainty is whether such trial-mediated application qualifies as **Operation of the bounded diagnostic capability**, or requires a separate non-trial implementation warrant. The independent CheXNet/Zech model/task reproduction-and-challenge offers a *contested* VS-04 interpretation; it is not a successful independent repeat of MASAI.
- **Disposition: DEFER to existing VSC-001**, with the existing October bounded review's explicit alternatives. Do not create a second generic stage-definition change, new study, or automatic downgrade. Request a separate operator ruling only on its documented case, with dissent and replication-target boundary retained.

### A03-05 — Public copy under-defines Operation and blurs “testing” with achieved evidence depth [ADAPT; HIGH confidence]
- [Public reading guide source](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/pages/Stubs.jsx), lines ~94–97, says **VS-05: “the claim is being tested in real-world conditions”**. Ratified July rule requires *sustained or applied operation of the claimed capability*, and distinguishes it from a functioning trial apparatus. Merely beginning a test does not necessarily reach Operation. The current [Methodology architecture index](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/pages/Methodology.jsx), lines ~131–136, says “operationally validated result”, potentially sounding more conclusive than the case-bounded “applied operation” standard. VS-04 guide phrasing “groups are attempting to reproduce the result” agrees with null/adversarial inclusion in principle but omits the *same defined target* condition.
- [Shared trajectory stage labels](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/data/trajectoryVisuals.js) correctly map VS-04/05 to Replication/Operation. [Record header, matrix and history](https://github.com/StuKeg1/faultline-observatory-site/blob/main/src/pages/FrontierRecord.jsx) do not equate stage and Pressure State; AI-0008's explicit interim under-review notice is a real safeguard. Therefore, **do not assert all stage presentation is inconsistent**.
- **Disposition: ADAPT a bounded reader-copy clarification proposal**, anchored verbatim to ratified July criteria and with counterexamples (failed reproduction can qualify VS-04; testing in the real world does not automatically qualify VS-05; operation of a capability can coexist with FRAGMENTING or other contested trajectory). No public copy edit now. Do not change FCIF definitions or implied threshold.
