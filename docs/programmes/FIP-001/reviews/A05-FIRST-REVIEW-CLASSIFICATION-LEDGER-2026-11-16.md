## Result and exact cohort definition

A complete review of **32 record modules** and the original review/correction mutations yields:

- **Core cycle D01–D31:** **31 distinct records**, each reviewed once between **2026-08-30 and 2026-09-28**. **26 first reviews identified corrections or source-representation discrepancies; 5 were source-faithful/clean or enrichment-only.** Rate on this *actual reviewed first cycle* = **26/31 (83.87%)**, *not* 26/32.
- **Additional first review D42:** **FR-AM-0008**, 2026-10-05, separately first-reviewed and **clean**. The *extended ever-first-reviewed sample* is **32**, with 26 discrepancy-positive and 6 clean records; **26/32 (81.25%)**. This extended denominator is useful for comparing to Claude's claim but spans a later review window and should not be labelled the D01–D31 cycle.
- **Material threshold from A05's pre-existing rule:** A source-representation correction is material when it changes the represented result, study method, independent corroboration, causal/warrant inference, attributable source, or *patient/cohort denominator*. On that rule, **26/31 core** and **26/32 extended** contain at least one material discrepancy, including D28. **Borderline sensitivity:** if D28's enrolled-versus-randomised participant correction is instead judged minor-only because it does not change the record's conclusion, the material-positive count becomes **25/31** and **25/32**. **No evidence in the first-review ledger establishes two separate minor-only records**, so Claude's **24/32 (75%)** material-only count is **not substantiated by this classification**.
- These are **records with a discrepancy identified in their original review**, NOT proportions of individual evidence instances that were inaccurate, NOT a count of presently unresolved corrections, NOT a direct measure of final scientific reliability, and NOT a publicly authorised Observatory error rate.
- **Original-cycle repair outcome:** every original discrepancy-positive record has a later or same-day bounded correction or explicit withdrawal trail in its canonical mutation history. Some legacy unverified attribution is intentionally retained as debt, and later second-cycle reviews may still find separate discrepancies; neither is erased by a first-cycle correction. **Detected** and **repaired** are distinct dated events.

### Classification terms

- **M:** First review identified a *material source-representation issue* under the current A05 threshold. A mix of material and minor issues still counts once as material-positive.
- **C:** First review source-faithful, or added provenance/citations without changing meaning, with no discrepancy found.
- **M*:** Material by the predefined *cohort-denominator* rule, but explicitly exposed to sensitivity analysis; do not silently relabel as minor.
- **Repaired:** Later or same-day first-review correction/withdrawal recorded; retains any explicit provenance limitation or later review debt. No assertion that every unrelated discrepancy in that record is now absent.
- **Review locator:** the record link opens the exact canonical source; original `mutationLog[]` entry references the D## review and its receipt, and correction entries reference the same D##. The original scientific source links and issue detail remain in those governed records. `M-` references below are used when verified from a documented retained receipt, not inferred from ordinal mutation positions.

## Explicit limits and next disposition

This is a **record-level original-review census**, not a full independent re-verification of each cited scientific paper and not a public postmortem on the scientific truth of claims. Severity classes are **editorial judgement under documented definitions**. Exact incident counts *per instance* and independent second-assessor agreement have not been measured, and historical source Git receipts were not individually diffed byte-for-byte for each correction in this pass. The `M*` D28 cohort-denominator decision requires explicit operator ratification before *any* outward-facing material-error statistic could be approved.

**Recommended A05 disposition:** evidence-only classification census **COMPLETE**, but **public report NOT AUTHORISED** and **severity-policy publication decision required**. Do not open a code repair or publish a rate. Proceed to FIP-D01 only after A05's disposition is explicitly recorded; preserve FIP-A02 historical MCP date parity as deferred and A04 as accepted.
