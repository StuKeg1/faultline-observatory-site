## Reader boundaries

The guide explicitly distinguishes papers, experimental observations, patients and research teams. Follow-up and peer-reviewed reporting can add value without becoming another independent replication. No universal independence count or score is introduced. The current State Warrant remains authoritative. The guide is dated; its reviewed assessment IDs and evidence-entry IDs are retained as internal editorial references, not live assessment substitutes.
