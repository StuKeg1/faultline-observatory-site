# Normal Record Review — FR-BT-0005 — Lancet xenokidney candidate

Date: 2026-09-26

## Decision

**ADMIT as IN-008.**

Riella et al., *Porcine kidney xenotransplantation as a bridge to allotransplantation: a first-in-human study*, The Lancet 408 (2026), 1029–1040. DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01295-X.

The paper is not merely a provenance upgrade to IN-003. It adds peer-reviewed mechanistic and clinical evidence: immediate xenograft function; 271 days of dialysis independence; reversible early T-cell-mediated rejection; later microvascular inflammation/endothelial injury progressing to thrombotic microangiopathy after immunosuppression reduction during non-zoonotic bacterial infection; macrophage/NK-cell-predominant late infiltrate; no detected porcine pathogen transmission; unchanged anti-HLA antibodies; and successful human allotransplantation 82 days after explantation with no evidence of sensitisation during 231 days follow-up.

## Assessment

AS-002 appended. Pressure State remains **ESCALATING**. Verification Stage remains **VS-03**.

The result strengthens the capability evidence from weeks to nearly nine months of dialysis-independent renal replacement and establishes a first bridge to subsequent human allotransplantation. It simultaneously strengthens the failure-mechanism evidence by directly resolving late microvascular/innate injury in this recipient. Prospective multi-recipient replication remains outstanding, so VS-04 is not reached.

IN-003 remains unchanged as the earlier chronology. LPR-001-D29 remains unchanged.
