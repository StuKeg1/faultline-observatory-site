**LaSc identity recovered.** Song et al., *Room-Temperature Superconductivity at 298 K in Ternary La-Sc-H System at High-pressure Conditions*, [arXiv:2510.01273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01273), [DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2510.01273](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.01273).
