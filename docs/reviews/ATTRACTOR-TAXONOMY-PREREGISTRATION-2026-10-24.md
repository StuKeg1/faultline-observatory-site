# ATTRACTOR Taxonomy Governance Review — Pre-registration

**Status:** PRE-REGISTERED — classification not started  
**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Frozen corpus commit:** `94dfc8ba721eca5bc71def192c4273f3b6d84266`  
**Test population:** the 30 canonical mechanisms typed `ATTRACTOR` at the frozen commit.  
**Purpose:** determine whether current ATTRACTOR practice contains reproducible governed subtypes or whether the mechanism class is semantically overloaded.

This review is an FCIF governance review. It is independent of the closed reader-interface prototype. It does not authorise UI work, schema additions, record repairs, reassessment, or new ATTRACTOR wording.

## 1. Candidate taxonomy

Classification is performed against these candidate categories. These are hypotheses to test, not ratified FCIF doctrine.

### P — Positive evidential path
A prospective evidence condition or development whose described successful occurrence would materially strengthen the tracked proposition or move it toward establishment.

Required characteristics:
1. It directly engages the governed proposition.
2. The object states a prospective condition/development rather than merely a topic to investigate.
3. Its successful occurrence is directionally positive for the tracked proposition.
4. It does not itself prescribe an automatic Pressure State or Verification Stage transition.

### D — Discriminating test
A prospective test, method, evidence-generating procedure, or bounded evidential condition whose result is materially capable of discriminating between support for and contestation of the tracked proposition, where the object itself does not predetermine which direction the result will take.

Required characteristics:
1. It directly engages a material uncertainty in the governed proposition.
2. The prospective test/condition is sufficiently bounded to recognise relevant future evidence.
3. Both materially supportive and materially contesting outcomes are genuinely possible under the object as written.
4. It does not itself prescribe an automatic Pressure State or Verification Stage transition.

A condition does **not** become D merely because any scientific experiment could in principle fail. The canonical object must itself frame the evidential route as discriminating or outcome-open.

### R — Reopening path
A prospective evidence condition or review route attached to a record whose existing governed trajectory has reached a bounded terminal or historical disposition, where satisfying the condition would justify reopening/revisiting that disposition rather than directly establishing the tracked proposition.

Required characteristics:
1. The current canonical record explicitly makes reopening/revisiting relevant.
2. The object specifies evidence or a bounded review condition capable of triggering that reconsideration.
3. Reopening is not treated as automatic confirmation of the tracked proposition.
4. Later successor evidence that does not engage the bounded historical claim is insufficient.

### U — Unclear / does not fit
Use U when an object cannot be assigned to exactly one of P, D or R from its canonical text under these criteria, including where:
- more than one category applies materially;
- classification requires importing surrounding corpus interpretation not contained in the object;
- the object is too vague to satisfy the relevant category;
- it behaves primarily as an Open Question, research direction, watch item, bottleneck, resistance mechanism, or generic next step rather than one of the candidate ATTRACTOR functions.

No forced best-fit classification is permitted.

## 2. Explicit exclusions

The candidate ATTRACTOR taxonomy excludes:

1. **Negative-convergence routes as such.** A prospective condition whose successful occurrence would materially weaken, falsify, collapse, or move the tracked proposition in the negative direction is not P. It is not D unless the same canonical object defines a genuinely outcome-open discriminating test. FR-AM-0002 OQ-004's route of repeated high-powered null results is the fixed counterexample: negative convergence alone must not qualify as an ATTRACTOR under this candidate taxonomy.
2. Generic unanswered scientific questions.
3. Generic "more evidence is needed" statements.
4. Research directions whose relevance is interesting but whose prospective evidential condition is not bounded enough to apply later.
5. Bottlenecks that merely identify what prevents adjudication.
6. Resistance mechanisms that explain why evidence may fail to converge.
7. Retrospective collapse mechanisms.
8. Automatic state/stage transition rules.
9. Commercial, scientific, regulatory, or technical milestones that do not materially engage the governed proposition.

These exclusions are part of the test. If existing ATTRACTOR objects cannot be distinguished from these excluded forms reproducibly, the taxonomy hypothesis fails.

## 3. ATTRACTOR / Open Question boundary

An Open Question records something the institution has retained as unresolved. It may ask what is unknown, what should be tested, what a term means, how evidence should be interpreted, or even state conditions that would answer the question.

For this review, an ATTRACTOR must do more than state an unresolved question or its possible answer. Its canonical object must encode a **prospective evidential route** with enough materiality and boundedness to function as one of P, D or R.

Therefore:
- An OQ can contain a resolution condition without thereby being an ATTRACTOR.
- A negative resolution condition remains an OQ/other governance object unless it independently satisfies D as an outcome-open discriminating route.
- A definitional question that must be settled before evidence can be judged is not an ATTRACTOR merely because resolving the definition would advance the record.
- If classifiers cannot apply this boundary reliably, that is evidence the ATTRACTOR class is overloaded or insufficiently governed.

## 4. Admission conditions

Every object assigned P, D or R must satisfy all four baseline admission conditions:

1. **Claim relevance** — directly engages the governed proposition.
2. **Materiality** — the prospective route could materially affect the evidential position of the record.
3. **Specificity** — bounded enough that future evidence can be assessed against it without inventing a new criterion.
4. **Non-automaticity** — does not predetermine reassessment, Pressure State, or Verification Stage; normal governed review remains necessary.

Failure of any baseline condition requires U.

## 5. Legacy-object rule

No existing ATTRACTOR is grandfathered.

The 30 objects are evidence of historical practice, not proof of correct doctrine. An existing object that fails the candidate taxonomy is classified U and, only if the taxonomy is subsequently ratified, becomes a candidate for a separate bounded consistency review. It is not automatically deleted, rewritten, retyped, or migrated.

Low-specificity legacy objects receive no relaxed standard.

## 6. Independent classification protocol

Two classifications will be performed independently over the same frozen 30-object population.

### Classifier 1
May have prior familiarity with Faultline and the exploratory ATTRACTOR audit. It must classify using only the committed criteria when making the recorded classification.

### Classifier 2 — clean
Must receive only:
- this committed pre-registration document; and
- the 30 canonical ATTRACTOR objects from the frozen corpus, identified by record ID/object ID and canonical object text.

Classifier 2 must not receive:
- Classifier 1 results;
- prior discussion or prototype history;
- expected classifications;
- prior ATTRACTOR audit conclusions;
- post-registration explanatory material.

Classifier 2 must classify each object as P, D, R or U and may cite only the committed criteria and supplied canonical object text.

Classifications must be frozen before comparison. Disagreements are not adjudicated before threshold evaluation.

## 7. Pre-registered thresholds

The subtype hypothesis passes the reproducibility test only if **all** conditions below hold:

1. **Exact agreement:** at least **26 of 30 objects (86.7%)** receive the same P/D/R/U classification from both classifiers.
2. **Unclear ceiling:** each classifier assigns **U to no more than 3 of 30 objects (10%)**.
3. **OQ-boundary viability:** neither classifier identifies **4 or more objects** for which the ATTRACTOR/Open Question boundary cannot be applied without a material judgement call beyond the committed criteria.
4. **Exclusion viability:** the committed exclusions must continue to exclude the fixed FR-AM-0002 negative-convergence counterexample. Any interpretation required to admit that negative route as an ATTRACTOR constitutes failure of the candidate taxonomy rather than grounds to broaden the definition during this review.

No post-hoc category redefinition, threshold change, best-fit recoding, or reconciliation may be used to turn a failed result into a pass.

### Interpretation
- **PASS:** all four conditions pass. This supports, but does not itself ratify, P/D/R as reproducible ATTRACTOR subtypes. A separate governance decision may then consider ratification.
- **FAIL:** any condition fails. ATTRACTOR remains unratified; evidence supports semantic overload or inadequate criteria.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the prescribed test cannot be completed faithfully within the time-box. The default outcome is the same operational hold as FAIL; it does not authorise an extension.

## 8. Time-box and expiry rule

The review is limited to:
1. extraction/freeze of the 30 canonical objects;
2. Classifier 1 classification;
3. one clean independent Classifier 2 classification;
4. mechanical comparison against the pre-registered thresholds;
5. a bounded results report.

No taxonomy repair iteration is permitted inside this review.

If the review cannot reach a valid PASS or FAIL after those steps, it closes **INCONCLUSIVE**. ATTRACTOR remains unratified. FR-AM-0001 and FR-AM-0002 ATTRACTOR repairs remain held. Reader-facing ATTRACTOR surfacing remains held. A further review would require a separately authorised scope and new pre-registration.

## 10. Closed prototype record

The preceding reader-interface architecture prototype is closed with this result:

> **CLOSED — DO NOT BUILD YET.** Existing canonical Frontier Record data cannot currently support a semantically stable, deterministic reader-facing ATTRACTOR feature without first resolving an independent FCIF taxonomy question.
