# A4 Institutional-Authority Consistency Repair — 2026-10-24

**Status:** BOUNDED CORPUS-INTEGRITY REPAIR  
**Scope:** FR-AM-0004 AT-001; FR-AM-0005 AT-001; FR-AM-0006 AT-001; FR-QE-0003 AT-001; FR-QE-0004 AT-001  
**Base:** `94dfc8ba721eca5bc71def192c4273f3b6d84266`

## Basis

Existing FCIF governance keeps Pressure State, Verification Stage, controlling proposition verdict and reopening under governed assessment authority. A prospective evidence route may materially engage or justify consideration of those decisions, but it does not perform them by its own terms.

The five scoped ATTRACTORs correctly identify prospective evidence routes but used wording that assigned the subsequent institutional consequence directly to the evidence event.

This repair is independent of ATTRACTOR taxonomy v2. It does not reinterpret, reopen or rescue the closed v2 FAIL, and it is not a v3 taxonomy attempt.

## Repairs

### FR-AM-0004 / AT-001
Replaced automatic transition wording with: the plant-level net-electricity event would **materially engage a governed reassessment of whether** the record should move from ESCALATING toward RESOLVING.

### FR-AM-0005 / AT-001
Replaced automatic reopening wording with: independent reproduction would **provide a bounded evidential basis for considering governed reopening**.

The reopening function is retained; the evidence event no longer performs the institutional reopening itself.

### FR-AM-0006 / AT-001
Replaced automatic transition wording with: qualifying commercial deployment would **materially engage a governed reassessment of whether** the record should move from ESCALATING toward RESOLVING.

### FR-QE-0003 / AT-001
Replaced wording that increasing-distance confirmation would **constitute resolution** with wording that it would **provide material evidence for a governed assessment of whether the claim has reached resolution**.

### FR-QE-0004 / AT-001
Replaced wording that the milestone would **move the claim** with wording that it would **materially strengthen the evidential basis for a governed assessment of whether** the claim should move beyond RESOLVING toward a fully confirmed state.
