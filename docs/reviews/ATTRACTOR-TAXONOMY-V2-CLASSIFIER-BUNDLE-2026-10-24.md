## 3. Admission rule

Admission is scored separately from subtype.

An object is **ADMIT** only if all four conditions below pass and no explicit exclusion applies.

### A1 — Claim relevance

The route directly engages the governed proposition.

The classifier receives the governed proposition verbatim as a permitted input. Claim relevance must be determined from the proposition, ATTRACTOR object and permitted explicit references only.

### A2 — Materiality

The prospective route could materially affect the evidential position of the governed proposition. Mere adjacency, programme progress or enabling activity is insufficient unless the object makes its material bearing on the proposition clear.

### A3 — Specificity

ATTRACTOR specificity governs **recognisability of future evidential engagement**, not automatic adjudication.

A prospective route is sufficiently specific only when a later reviewer can identify:

1. **Target** — the phenomenon, capability, relationship, result or uncertainty future evidence engages.
2. **Evidence form** — the material kind of observation, test, replication, demonstration, comparison or development.
3. **Material scope/boundary** — enough contextual constraint to distinguish qualifying engagement from merely adjacent progress.

Operational test:

> Could a later reviewer determine whether new evidence materially engages the recorded route using only the ATTRACTOR and any explicit, uniquely resolvable canonical references it contains, without inventing a new target, evidence form or material boundary?

Numeric thresholds are not inherently required. Qualitative terms may be sufficient when target, evidence form and material boundary make the route recognisable; a qualitative word cannot carry the criterion by itself.

#### Canonical-reference rule for Specificity

A reference may supply missing specificity only if it is:
- explicit;
- uniquely resolvable;
- present in the frozen canonical snapshot;
- materially relevant to the missing target/evidence-form/boundary information;
- usable without editorial inference or choosing among plausible contextual meanings.

Missing, ambiguous, stale or non-resolving references fail closed.

### A4 — Non-automaticity

ATTRACTOR may describe expected evidential significance and direction, but it may not make occurrence of the future condition sufficient by itself to determine a governed institutional outcome.

Operational test:

> After the stated future condition occurs, does FCIF still require a separate governed decision to determine Pressure State, Verification Stage, controlling assessment/verdict, reopening action or mandatory review procedure?

If yes, the wording may pass Non-automaticity.

If no, it fails.

If genuinely ambiguous, it fails closed.

Institutional premises supplied to both classifiers:
- Pressure State, Verification Stage and the controlling proposition verdict are governed assessment outputs.
- Declaring the tracked proposition established, rejected or resolved as Faultline's controlling judgment is a governed assessment act.
- ATTRACTOR has no standing authority to mandate reassessment, reopening, review cadence or another institutional workflow.
- Directional evidential language such as "strengthen", "weaken", "support", "toward", "warrant" or "justify" is not itself automatic.
- A separate governed decision must remain substantively necessary, not merely ceremonial.
- Ambiguous default or presumptive transition language fails closed.

## 4. Explicit admission exclusions

Reject admission when the object is primarily any of the following rather than an admitted prospective evidential route:

1. A negative-convergence route as such. A route whose successful occurrence is solely defined as weakening/falsifying/collapsing the proposition is not P and is not admitted as D unless the same object is genuinely outcome-open.
2. A generic unanswered scientific question.
3. Generic "more evidence is needed".
4. A research direction that lacks sufficient target/evidence-form/material-boundary specificity.
5. A bottleneck that merely identifies what prevents adjudication.
6. A resistance mechanism explaining why evidence may fail to converge.
7. A retrospective collapse mechanism.
8. An automatic State/Stage/verdict/reopening/review rule.
9. A commercial, scientific, regulatory or technical milestone that does not materially engage the governed proposition.

Fixed exclusion check retained from v1:

> FR-AM-0002 OQ-004's negative-convergence route of repeated high-powered null results is not an ATTRACTOR merely because it could resolve uncertainty negatively. It is not P and is not D unless the same route is genuinely outcome-open.

If the registered criteria require that fixed negative-only route to be admitted, the admission taxonomy fails.

## 5. ATTRACTOR / Open Question boundary

An Open Question records something retained as unresolved. It may itself state a condition that would answer the question.

An ATTRACTOR must do more: it must encode a prospective evidential route satisfying A1–A4.

Therefore:
- an OQ may contain a resolution condition without thereby being an ATTRACTOR;
- a negative resolution condition remains OQ/other unless the same route independently qualifies as an outcome-open D;
- a definitional prerequisite is not an ATTRACTOR merely because resolving the definition would advance the record;
- wording framed as a question is not automatically excluded if the object itself still encodes an admissible prospective evidential route;
- if the prospective route cannot be distinguished from an unresolved question using the supplied proposition, ATTRACTOR text and permitted references, admission fails closed.

## 6. Subtype taxonomy

Subtype is evaluated **only after ADMIT**.

### P — Positive evidential path

A prospective route whose encoded function is to supply materially positive evidence toward establishment of the governed proposition while leaving the controlling verdict to governed assessment.

Required:
1. directionally positive evidential function;
2. prospective condition/development rather than generic topic;
3. passes A1–A4;
4. not more specifically R or D under the functional rules below.

### D — Discriminating test

A prospective test, method, evidence-generating procedure or bounded evidential condition whose encoded function is outcome-open discrimination between materially supportive and materially contesting outcomes.

Required:
1. directly engages a material uncertainty in the proposition;
2. both supportive and contesting outcomes are genuinely possible under the route as written;
3. the object itself frames the route as discriminating/outcome-open; mere possibility that an experiment could fail is insufficient;
4. passes A1–A4.

### R — Reopening path

A prospective route whose encoded institutional function is to make a bounded prior disposition eligible for reopening/revisiting through governed action.

Required:
1. the ATTRACTOR itself, or a permitted explicit canonical reference, makes reopening/revisiting the relevant function;
2. the condition supplies a bounded evidential route for that reconsideration;
3. reopening is not automatic confirmation and remains a governed action;
4. successor or adjacent evidence that does not engage the bounded proposition is insufficient;
5. passes A1–A4.

A classifier may not infer terminal/closed status from broader record knowledge not supplied in the permitted bundle.

### Functional precedence

For an admitted object:

1. If the route explicitly functions to reopen/revisit a bounded prior disposition, classify **R**.
2. Otherwise, if the route is explicitly outcome-open/discriminating, classify **D**.
3. Otherwise, if it is directionally positive toward establishment, classify **P**.
4. If more than one subtype remains materially applicable after these functional distinctions, return **SUBTYPE-UNCLEAR**.

Scientific strength does not turn R into P. If wording makes occurrence sufficient to establish the proposition as Faultline's controlling verdict, that is an A4 failure, not a P/R tie-breaker.
