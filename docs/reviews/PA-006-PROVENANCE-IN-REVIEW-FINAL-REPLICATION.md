# PA-006 — Provenance-in-Review Final Replication Trial

**Target:** FR-BT-0002 — Epigenetic Reprogramming — Biological Age Reversal Without Identity Loss  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Status:** EXECUTED — pending PR / deployment verification

## Purpose

Final replication of the provenance-in-review operating procedure in a third programme (Biotechnology), following PA-004 and PA-005.

## Result

A normal Record Review identified a material operational development: ER-100 progressed from IND clearance to first-participant dosing in its Phase 1 human trial. IN-007 was admitted with structured `sources[]` at admission. AS-003 retains ESCALATING / VS-02 because no human outcome data have yet been reported.

Opportunistic legacy enrichment was also performed on IN-006. The original IND-clearance event was immediately verifiable against Life Biosciences' primary announcement and ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07290244. Structured provenance was added without changing the historical description or assessment. Unlike PA-004 and PA-005, this check found no material legacy factual or classification discrepancy.

## Trial observation

PA-006 therefore replicates the low-friction provenance workflow while providing an important negative control on the error-detection effect: provenance enrichment can verify a legacy instance cleanly rather than necessarily generating a correction. No systematic backfill was attempted.

## Governance implication

The three-trial series is now sufficient for a separate governance decision on whether provenance capture should become standard Record Review procedure.
