## Question

Given that ATTRACTOR remains an unratified working term and the registered v2 review closed FAIL with no v3, what should FCIF do with the existing legacy objects without creating a replacement taxonomy?

This review is not an ATTRACTOR admission test. It does not reuse P/D/R as a governed subtype system and does not attempt to make the failed taxonomy pass.

## Permitted dispositions

1. **RETAIN AS WORKING METADATA** — retain the legacy object as non-constitutional explanatory metadata. It does not control State Warrant, Pressure State, Verification Stage, verdict, reopening or review cadence.
2. **RECLASSIFY UNDER EXISTING FCIF STRUCTURE** — only where the content clearly belongs to an already-governed structure without inventing a new category.
3. **RETIRE** — remove the object only where it adds no institutional information not already represented elsewhere.

No new category, subtype or negative-attractor field may be created by this review.

## Corpus-wide disposition

The current legacy population is retained as **working metadata by default**. The failed taxonomy does not itself justify rewriting or deleting legacy corpus content. Existing ATTRACTOR objects remain explanatory mechanisms with no constitutional authority and no presumption that every record should contain one.

The v2 result is retained only as diagnostic evidence: both classifiers agreed on all 30 admission calls and jointly rejected seven legacy objects, but the registered historical-population coherence ceiling failed. That result cannot be converted post hoc into a corpus admission rule.

The five A4 institutional-authority defects among the jointly rejected objects — FR-AM-0004, FR-AM-0005, FR-AM-0006, FR-QE-0003 and FR-QE-0004 — have already been independently corrected on FCIF authority grounds in PR #198 / `fb96a73a...`. ## Two remaining unanimous-reject objects

### FR-AI-0001 / AT-001 — RETIRE CANDIDATE, NOT EXECUTED

AT-001 describes extended chain-of-thought as a mechanism candidate and explicitly says whether it produces genuine generalisation or higher-order pattern matching is an open research question.

Current OQ-002 already asks whether o1/o3-class computation supports generalisation beyond memorised or structurally familiar examples, what mechanism accounts for the performance, and separately whether verbalised chain-of-thought faithfully describes the computation.

**Finding:** AT-001 does not currently supply a distinct institutional function beyond the governed open-question structure. Retirement is independently plausible because of duplication, not because v2 rejected it.

**Disposition:** retain unchanged pending a bounded redundancy review. Do not reclassify automatically: OQ-002 already exists, so converting AT-001 into another Open Question would duplicate rather than clarify the record.

### FR-BT-0001 / AT-001 — RETIRE/REWRITE CANDIDATE, NOT EXECUTED

AT-001 identifies two enabling developments: an FDA aging/geroscience indication framework and a validated biomarker panel. These developments could make healthspan trials more tractable and reduce BN-001's measurement/threshold problem, but they are not themselves evidence that senolytics meaningfully extend human healthspan.

**Finding:** the object is useful context about measurement and trial infrastructure, but its present placement as a prospective resolution mechanism can blur enabling conditions with claim-bearing evidence. Existing BN-001 is the natural governed structure for the measurement-threshold problem; however, the current AT-001 also contains forward-looking information not necessarily duplicated verbatim by BN-001.

**Disposition:** retain unchanged pending a bounded mechanism-overlap review. Do not reclassify or retire solely from this review.

## Records without ATTRACTOR

### FR-AM-0001 — ABSENCE ACCEPTED; HOLD CLOSED AS NO ACTION REQUIRED

OQ-002 already states the bounded reopening evidence: reproducible nuclear products attributable to the electrochemical cell itself at or near room temperature, without an external ion beam or equivalent high-energy driver, with controls establishing nuclear energy and byproduct balance.

No ATTRACTOR object is required. The previous idea of adding one is closed unless a future independent governance need arises.

### FR-AM-0002 — ABSENCE ACCEPTED; HOLD CLOSED AS NO ACTION REQUIRED

OQ-004 already states both positive and negative convergence routes. Positive convergence requires a shared discriminating protocol plus independent replication of a prespecified excess-heat result with complete chemical-energy accounting; repeated high-powered null results under the same agreed conditions would provide negative convergence.

Forcing that material into ATTRACTOR would revive the positive/negative asymmetry problem and is not justified while ATTRACTOR is unratified.

### FR-QE-0002 — ABSENCE ACCEPTED; NO CHANGE REQUIRED

The record retains unresolved questions about the classical comparison class and the evaluative meaning of practical advantage. Absence of an ATTRACTOR is not a schema defect and no prospective route should be invented merely for symmetry.
