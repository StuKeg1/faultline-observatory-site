# Normal Record Review — FR-BT-0004

Date: 2026-09-26
Origin: two new-evidence candidates held outside LPR-001-D28.

## Candidate 1 — Sasieni et al., NEJM

**Decision: ADMIT as IN-007.**

Sasieni et al., *Effect of Screening with Multicancer Early-Detection Test on Late-Stage Cancer Diagnosis*, New England Journal of Medicine, published 22 September 2026, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2505723.

This is the peer-reviewed primary randomized NHS-Galleri result. It supersedes conference-only publication status as the strongest source for the trial outcome but does not erase IN-006's historical ASCO event. Primary Stage III/IV endpoint: IRR 1.03 (95% CI 0.92–1.14; P=0.63). Key Stage IV secondary endpoint: IRR 0.86 (95% CI 0.74–1.00). Mortality remains unresolved.

## Candidate 2 — Neal et al., Nature Medicine

**Decision: ADMIT as IN-008.**

Neal et al., *Performance of a multi-cancer early detection test in the randomized controlled NHS-Galleri trial*, Nature Medicine, published 22 September 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04652-8.

This is distinct evidence rather than a duplicate of the primary RCT publication: it reports prespecified three-round test-performance endpoints. PPV: 58.0%, 50.4%, 45.8%; specificity: 99.50–99.60%; all-cancer episode sensitivity: 37.2%, 27.1%, 26.7%. The analysis is descriptive. Mortality and overdiagnosis are reserved for future reporting.

## Assessment

AS-003 appended. Pressure State remains **FRAGMENTING**. Verification Stage remains **VS-04**. The publications materially strengthen evidence quality and characterize repeated-screening performance, but they do not satisfy AT-001's requirement for randomized mortality evidence without disproportionate overdiagnosis or downstream harm.

No historical assessment was rewritten. LPR-001-D28 remains unchanged.
