# FR-AM-0006 — VS-03 Classification Correction

Status: EXECUTED — pending CI / production verification
Date: 2026-08-29

## Finding

The bounded classification review found that AS-002's move to VS-03 rested on a mischaracterised source event. Toyota's relevant Japanese-government event was METI certification of a battery development and production plan under the Battery Supply Assurance Plan on 2024-09-06, not an October 2025 independent regulatory or technical approval of Toyota's solid-state battery performance.

## Correction

- IN-006 and the matching lineage wording are factually corrected.
- AS-002 and AS-003 remain immutable historical assessments.
- AS-004 is appended with ESCALATING retained and current Verification Stage corrected to VS-02.
- The correction is epistemic, not evidence of technological deterioration.

## Boundary

No claim is made that METI certification is irrelevant. It is meaningful institutional/industrial evidence, but it does not provide the independent claim-level scrutiny required for VS-03. No unrelated evidence or provenance backfill is performed.
